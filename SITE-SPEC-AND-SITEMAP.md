# Tidewater Swim Academy: Site Specification and Sitemap

## Site Specification

**Website Name:** Tidewater Swim Academy  
**Location:** Nyali, Mombasa  
**Website Type:** Swimming academy website

### Purpose

Tidewater Swim Academy is a fictional swimming school based in Nyali, Mombasa. The purpose of the website is to provide clear information about the academy, its swimming programs, fees and coaching services. The website also allows potential clients to contact the academy and book a free trial session.

### Target Audience

The website is designed for:

- Parents looking for swimming lessons for their children
- Adult beginners who want to learn how to swim
- Aspiring competitive swimmers looking for structured training

### Website Objectives

The website aims to:

- Introduce visitors to Tidewater Swim Academy
- Provide information about available swimming programs and fees
- Present information about the academy and its coaches
- Help visitors easily contact the academy
- Allow potential clients to request a free trial session
- Provide a simple and responsive experience across desktop and mobile devices

### Design

The website uses a calm, professional, water-inspired design that reflects swimming and the coastal location of the academy. The design focuses on clear navigation, readable content, appropriate imagery and consistent styling across all pages.

### Main Features

- Navigation menu linking all four pages
- Information about swimming programs and fees
- Information about the academy and coaching team
- Contact and free-trial booking form
- Swimming-related images and visual content
- Responsive layout for different screen sizes
- Consistent colors, typography and page styling

## Sitemap

```text
Home (index.html)
├── Programs & Fees (services.html)
├── Our Story (about.html)
└── Contact & Booking (contact.html)
```

All four pages are connected through the main navigation menu, allowing users to move easily between different sections of the website.
