Why Tidewater

A swim school built around water safety and real progress.

Two thirds of the families who walk through our gate come to us for one reason: their child cannot yet be safe in water. We start there. Every learner — four years old or forty — works through the same clear milestones, so progress is something you can see rather than hope for.

  • Certified coaches with lifesaving and first-aid credentials
  • Maximum of four swimmers per coach in learn-to-swim lanes
  • Written milestone reports every six weeks
  • Warm 25 m indoor pool, open year round

Three pathways

Find the lane that matches where you are today.

How joining works

Four steps from enquiry to your first lap.

  1. Book a free trial

    Send the booking form and we reply within one business day with two session options.

  2. In-water assessment

    A coach spends 20 minutes with the swimmer to check comfort, breathing and stroke basics.

  3. Lane placement

    We recommend a group, a schedule and a six-week milestone target in writing.

  4. Start training

    Term fees are paid monthly, and you can switch groups whenever the swimmer is ready.
