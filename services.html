Ages 4–6

Little Tides

KSh 6,500 / month

Water confidence, submerging, floating and first kicks in shallow water with two coaches per lane.

  • 2 sessions weekly · 30 minutes
  • Maximum 6 swimmers
Ages 7–12

Learn to Swim

KSh 7,800 / month

A structured five-level pathway covering all four strokes, turns and 100 m continuous swimming.

  • 2 sessions weekly · 45 minutes
  • Milestone report every 6 weeks
Adults

Adult Beginners

KSh 8,400 / month

For adults who never learned, or who are anxious in deep water. Quiet lanes, slow build, no spectators.

  • 2 sessions weekly · 60 minutes
  • Maximum 5 swimmers
Adults

Stroke Clinic

KSh 9,600 / month

Technique repair for adults who can already swim: breathing rhythm, catch, body position and open-water pacing.

  • Weekly session · 60 minutes
  • Video review once a month
Squad

Performance Squad

KSh 14,500 / month

Periodised training for club and school competitors, with pace sets, dryland work and full meet preparation.

  • 5 sessions weekly · 90 minutes
  • Entry by coach assessment
One to one

Private Coaching

KSh 2,800 / session

A single coach, a single swimmer and one goal — a stubborn stroke fault, a start, a turn, or a time to beat.

  • Booked per session · 45 minutes
  • Available to all members

Questions

Before you book

No. Little Tides and Level 1 of Learn to Swim both start with swimmers who have never entered a pool. The assessment simply tells us which lane to place them in.

Each level has written milestones. Coaches assess every six weeks and send a short report; swimmers move up as soon as the milestones are met, whatever the term dates say.

Memberships can be paused once per term with two weeks’ notice, and transferred to another family member at any time at no cost.

Every coach holds a recognised swim teaching qualification, current lifesaving certification and first aid training, and a lifeguard is on deck for all sessions.
